## Image Guidelines

### Style & Theme
- **Luxury & Premium**: All images should convey high-end, exclusive, premium quality
- **Professional**: Clean, modern, sophisticated aesthetic
- **Aspirational**: Images should inspire and create desire
- **KoalaVault Brand**: Consider incorporating gold/black color themes where appropriate

### Technical Requirements
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High resolution, optimized for web
- **Aspect Ratios**: Maintain specified dimensions for proper layout
- **File Size**: Balance quality with loading speed (recommend <500KB per image)

### Suggested Sources
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) - Free high-quality stock photos
- **Pexels** (pexels.com) - Free stock photography
- **Shutterstock** (shutterstock.com) - Premium stock photos
- **Adobe Stock** (stock.adobe.com) - Professional stock imagery
