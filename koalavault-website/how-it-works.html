Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about KoalaVault

How are winners selected?

Winners are selected through a completely random and transparent process. We use certified random number generation systems and all draws are independently audited to ensure complete fairness.

Is my personal information secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption and never share your personal information with third parties. All payment processing is handled through secure, certified payment gateways.

How are prizes delivered?

Prize delivery varies by type. Cash prizes are transferred directly to your bank account, while physical prizes are delivered by secure courier. Our team coordinates all aspects of prize fulfillment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods through our secure payment partners including Stripe. All transactions are processed securely and encrypted.

Can I participate from anywhere?

KoalaVault is available in most countries worldwide. However, some restrictions may apply based on local regulations. Please check our terms and conditions for specific eligibility requirements.

Is there a limit to how many times I can enter?

Each opportunity has its own terms and entry limits. Some allow multiple entries while others limit to one per person. All details are clearly stated on each opportunity page.
